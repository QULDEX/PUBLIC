## About QULDEX

QULDEX™ is an Enterprise Audit Automation, Compliance and Governance, Risk & Compliance (GRC) Platform developed by EGV Group.

It helps organizations, GRC consultants, internal and external auditors, certification bodies, and enterprise teams manage audit programs, map controls across frameworks, collect evidence, track findings, coordinate corrective actions and produce audit reports.

QULDEX is designed to reduce repetitive audit activities through unified workflows, reusable control mappings, centralized evidence management and traceable remediation.

## Platform Capabilities

- **Audit Workspace:** Plan, schedule, execute, review and close audits.
- **Multi-Framework Audit Engine:** Map and assess common controls across applicable frameworks.
- **Control Library:** Organize controls and associate them with audit requirements.
- **Secure Evidence Vault:** Collect, organize and manage audit evidence with governed access.
- **Risk Register:** Record, assess, assign and track risks and treatment activities.
- **Findings & CAPA:** Manage nonconformities, corrective actions, owners, deadlines and verification.
- **Audit Reporting & Analytics:** Monitor audit progress, reporting and management visibility.
- **Auditor Management:** Coordinate audit responsibilities and multi-auditor activities.
